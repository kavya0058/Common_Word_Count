1.Takes a text and processes each word one by one.
2.Converts all words into lowercase for easy comparison.
3.Removes punctuation marks such as commas and periods.
4.Then splits the text into individual words.
5.Counts how many times each word appears in the text.
6.Stores the word and its count as key-value pairs.
7.Sorts the words based on their frequency.
8.The most frequently occurring words come first.
9.Returns the top 3 most common words.
10.Displays the words along with their occurrence count.
